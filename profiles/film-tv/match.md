# Film & TV Profile

Use this profile when an item's primary value is understanding film, television, filmmakers, studios, streamers, or the film/TV industry, rather than a general cultural or lifestyle topic.

Typical items include:
- Major film releases, premieres, or distribution deals
- Television series orders, renewals, cancellations, or showrunner changes
- Film festival premieres and awards (e.g., Cannes, Venice, Berlin, Sundance, TIFF)
- Awards season news (Oscars, Emmys, BAFTAs, Golden Globes)
- Box-office performance and streaming viewership data with meaningful baselines
- Casting, directing, writing, or producing attachments on significant projects
- Studio, streamer, or production company strategy and restructuring
- Industry labor news (guild negotiations, strikes, residuals, AI-and-labor disputes)
- Film and TV criticism with substantive engagement with form or craft
- Notable trends in production, distribution, exhibition, or streaming windows
- Significant legal disputes, rights acquisitions, or intellectual-property battles in film/TV
- Major franchise developments (sequels, reboots, cinematic universes)
- Documentaries and non-fiction filmmaking with clear artistic or industry significance

Route items about general celebrity gossip, red-carpet fashion, lifestyle coverage of actors, or reality-TV personalities to a general culture or lifestyle profile. Do not route routine casting rumors without confirmed attachments, promotional press releases without substantive news, or generic "what to watch this weekend" listicles.

For technology-related film/TV, route items about streaming infrastructure, AI video generation tools, or VFX pipeline software to the technology profile. Route significant AI-generated film projects, major VFX studio deals, or landmark virtual-production achievements here if they have clear artistic or industrial significance.
